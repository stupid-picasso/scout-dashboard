## 1. Method, access, limits (read this first)

**Reddit was not reachable.** `old.reddit.com`, `www.reddit.com` `.json` and a Pushshift-style mirror (pullpush) all returned 403/429 from this environment, and WebFetch refuses reddit.com. Redlib/libreddit mirrors (redlib.ssps.io, lr.us.psf.lt) either do not resolve from this session or returned 503. pogo.gamepress.gg is also outside the session's allowed domains. So **no r/pokemongo, r/TheSilphRoad, r/PokemonGOBattleLeague thread was read directly by me.** Where a Reddit thread is mentioned, it is either a mirror URL that appeared in search results [S] or a Reddit comment quoted by a news article I did open [O] (Dexerto, MeinMMO). Treat "how often it appears" as news-and-blog frequency, not Reddit frequency.

Also blocked (HTTP 403 to WebFetch): massivelyop.com, esports.gg, sportskeeda.com, dexerto.com (one URL worked, another 525), pokemongohub.net (one page worked, one 403). Their content comes only through the search tool's summaries [S].

**What I did:** about 30 web searches (topics: storage/transfer, stardust/power-up, GBL, Max Battles, raids/remote passes, Mega energy, event pricing, events/schedule, IV checkers, Calcy/Poke Genie/PvPoke/Pokebattler/Dittobase, HOME, outages) and about 25 page fetches. **Pages actually read [O]: 17** (listed in the source lists with [O]). **Further results seen only as search summaries [S]: about 110 links**, of which I cite roughly 45.

**Date range:** target was 2025-2026. Many [O] pages are 2024-2025; the ones that are 2026: MeinMMO spawn article (13 Jan 2026), MeinMMO catch-difficulty article (5 Aug 2026), Unstar raid-pass analysis (Sep 2026), GamingHQ Mega Finale (6 Sep 2026), plus the three promo-code pages (Nov 2026). Older [O] sources are used only where the complaint is a standing one and are flagged with their year.

**Caveats:** (a) Several search summaries are generated by the search tool and can be imprecise; I flagged what I could. (b) The Unstar figures are a third-party analysis of Android reviews. (c) App-store review text for Poke Genie / Calcy IV was reached only through aggregators [S], with undated quotes. (d) I found no direct PvPoke or Pokebattler user complaint threads. (e) "Count" in the table = number of distinct sources in this research that mention it, not a statistical frequency.

## 4. Best features to build (max 10, ordered by players affected x feasibility)

1. **Dust allocator ("spend my Stardust where")** - affects pain #7, #17, #12. Given dust, candy, XL and goals (GL/UL/raid/Max), rank the next ten power-ups by marginal value per 1,000 Stardust, and show "do not power" items (low-rank, no use). Plugs into the existing power-up/dust plan and Candy modules, using the A05/A06-verified cost tables and PvP ranks.
2. **Cup build-next shortlist** - #17, #14. For each active GBL cup, list the owned Pokemon that fit and the best two missing targets with total dust cost to reach rank N. Plugs into the PvP squad builder and Today cup list.
3. **In-game search-string generator for transfers** - #5, #6. From the Keep list emit one or more strings for the game's own search box (e.g. by name, age, CP band, star count) plus a preview of how many roster entries each string hits and a warning when it would also match a keeper. Plugs into Roster filters / Recs transfer lists.
4. **Guided "appraise these 10"** - #15, #14. A queue of the Pokemon whose league rank is most uncertain, with one-tap capture of the appraisal screenshot. Raises IV uniqueness above 0.4%. Plugs into Import and the IV solver.
5. **Shiny/lucky/shadow/regional dex grid** - #21, #20. Grid dex with flags and "missing" filters, from roster data already held. Plugs into Intel (Living Dex counters).
6. **Max Particle and Candy XL budget** - #8, #10. User enters particles; app shows daily cap, storage cap, days to afford Level 2/3 for each owned Max-capable Pokemon, with costs reconciled to game master. Plugs into the Max planner. Verify the 800/day and 1,000 caps (from 2024 reports) before shipping.
7. **Mega Energy source planner** - #18. Energy needed vs held, best source this week parsed from event text (Spotlight/raid/research). Plugs into Mega tracking and the Today event parser.
8. **Raid attacker simulation (Phase 6) with shadow-vs-normal dust cost** - #12. Boss movesets from game master, enrage timer, ranked "power up before it leaves". Plugs into Raid and Attackers. Hard; labelled as the planned last step.
9. **"What changed" data diff** - #4, #24. After each weekly refresh show moves/stat changes that affect the user's ranked Pokemon. Plugs into the data refresh workflows and Today health block.
10. **Pass-loss evidence kit** - #2, #23. One screen in Log that records date, boss, item and a screenshot, and produces a ready support message. Small, ToS-clean, gives players something to do in the 2026 raid-pass failures (Unstar: 63 of 1,324 detailed negatives, 4.8%).

## 5. Do not build

- **Anything that auto-catches, auto-spins, auto-raids, scans the map or reads wild Pokemon IVs from the client/server** (the "PokeAlert"-style scanners). Violates Niantic ToS; also login-based IV checkers are the ones that lead to bans (iMore [S]).
- **Spoofing, GPS faking, "remote Max Battle" workarounds.** ToS. Players ask for relief from Remote Raid limits and Max lobbies; the only legal fix is in-game.
- **Live IV overlay reading the running game.** Calcy/Poke Genie do this on-device on Android with the developer asserting ToS compliance [S], but it needs screen-capture permissions the PWA cannot get on iOS and it is a continuing ToS grey area; stay screenshot/recording based.
- **Reading the user's account (Niantic or Google login) to sync Pokemon.** ToS risk and exactly what players fear.
- **Raid/lobby finder or "who has a code" matchmaking.** Needs a server and moderation; not free tier.
- **Pass or coin refund automation, any support bot posing as Niantic.** Not possible.
- **Push notifications while the app is closed.** Needs a paid or self-run server; keep calendar export instead.
- **Spawn, shiny rate or catch-rate "analysis".** Rates are server-side; any numbers would be speculation. Do not publish claims about quiet nerfs.
- **Paid-API features** (large vision models, paid map or event data).

**Codes not on LeekDuck's page:**
- Insider Gaming: `LRQEV2VZ59UDA`, `DJTLEKBK2G5EK`, `P2XEAW56TSLUXH3` (listed as "try these"; Pocket Tactics lists all three as expired) and `HELPPOKEMON` (appears on no other page, no reward given; unverified, likely stale or invalid).
- Insider Gaming marks `0004POKEMONGO` (expired 22 Sep 2026 per LeekDuck) and `FENDIxFRGMTxPOKEMON` (expired per LeekDuck) as active: **stale entries**.
- Pocket Tactics: about 55 older expired codes LeekDuck lacks (e.g. GOFEST2024, STARPIECE2025, POKEBALL50, many 13-character research codes). All expired, no rewards, no value.
